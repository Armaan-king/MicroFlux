# Report — neural comparison, corrected, and first replication (2026-09-13)

Three bodies of evidence, kept apart. Every interval is a paired block
bootstrap on the validation (or test) window with the block count stated;
seed variability is the standard deviation of validation NLL across training
seeds and is a different quantity from sampling uncertainty. Neither is a
confidence interval across market sessions.

## 3. Fresh session 1 — `BTCUSDT-2026-09-09-early`

2.00 h (05:04–07:04 UTC, no overlap with §2), 22,134 orders; train 1.40 h,
validation 0.30 h (≈ 3,300 events), test 0.30 h (2,779 events).
Artifacts: `runs/BTCUSDT-2026-09-09-early/`.

Blocks behind each interval: **18 (60 s) / 4 (300 s) / 2 (900 s)**. The 300 s
and 900 s intervals are **insufficient for inference** on windows this short
(PROTOCOL.md §7) and are reported for completeness only.

**Classical replication** (`replication.md`): H1 — marks — **confirmed**,
+0.1416 [+0.126, +0.158] at 60 s, control −0.0006 ± 0.0014. The ladder's
spine replicates (Hawkes over Poisson +0.78; M4 over M1 +0.14). Not confirmed
as registered: H2 (direction holds — 1-fill fast share 0.26/0.29, 20+
0.91/0.85 — strict monotonicity fails on SELL), H3 (state in kernel
−0.0031 [−0.007, +0.002]), H4 (underpowered), H5 (BUY monotone; SELL
0.515/0.340/0.348), H6, H7, H8, H9. E1′ is *worse* than M4 here (−0.0010).
Reference for the neural stage = M4 (−0.0007). Rate over-prediction on
validation is present again (residual mean 1.17 vs null 1.03).

**Conclusion for this session: the neural advantage was not replicated here.**
Validation gains are within sampling uncertainty; on the untouched test
segment both neural models score below the classical reference. This is a
valid result. It does not show that summaries are useless, that attention is
ineffective, or that the amount of training data caused it; it shows that on
this session, under the frozen configuration, the classical reference was not
beaten.

PROTOCOL.md §7 requires the direction on ≥ 2 fresh sessions with intervals
clear of zero. One fresh session has been evaluated and did not meet it.

**Recommendation: retain the classical model as the working reference; the
attention result is inconclusive.** The exploratory-session gain is real on
that session and robust to the training seed, and it has not been seen on
independent data. The next experiment answers one question: *does the
attention model's validation gain over the classical reference on an
8-hour session appear on an independent 8-hour session, and does it hold on
that session's untouched test segment?* That is collection batch 1
(`docs/collection-batch-1.md`): two further 8-hour BTCUSDT captures on
different UTC dates, evaluated in capture order under the frozen protocol,
whatever they show. Continuous marks, richer book inputs, book-history
encoders and any tuning wait until that batch is complete.
